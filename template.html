    Metric definitions

    One definition per metric, used everywhere

    Applicant
    Unique domestic person applying for a first-semester undergraduate place
    Offer rate
    Applicants with an offer ÷ applicants
    Lead
    Unique CRM enquiry, duplicates removed
    Application
    Lead with status Applied, Offer or Enrolled
    CPL / CPA
    Paid spend ÷ leads / applications
    Click rate
    Unique clicks ÷ delivered emails
    Unattributed
    Lead with no source or UTM tag
